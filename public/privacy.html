Privacy

Last updated 17 August 2026

The short version. We do not have a server, we do not have accounts, and we do not run analytics or advertising. Everything you record in the tracker stays in your own browser. We could not identify you if we wanted to.

What stays on your device, always

  • The nickname you give each reciter.
  • Which books are colored, their status, the dates and your favourite surahs.
  • Your ayah notes and which days you practised.
  • Your licence key, and whether the free week has started.

All of it is kept in your browser’s local storage. None of it is transmitted to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it — from your device and from existence, because we never had a copy.

What leaves your device

Exactly one thing: when you unlock the app, and about once a week afterwards, your licence key is sent to Lemon Squeezy to check that it is still valid. Nothing else goes with it — no name, no email, no progress, no reciters. If that check cannot be made, the app carries on as normal.

If you buy a subscription, Lemon Squeezy collects your payment and billing details directly. They are the merchant of record and their privacy policy covers that information. We can see order records in their dashboard; we never see your card details.

The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which like any web host records requests to its servers.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies, and there are no tracking scripts, pixels or advertising networks on this site. There is nothing to consent to and no banner to dismiss.

Children

The app is for anyone memorizing the Quran, at any age, and is often used by families together. Because it asks for nothing but a nickname and stores everything locally, we do not knowingly hold personal information about anybody — child or adult. Please use a nickname rather than a full name.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data are hard for us to be asked for, because we hold almost none: your progress is already entirely in your hands, and you can delete it by clearing the app’s data or your browser. For anything held by Lemon Squeezy about a purchase, contact them or email us and we will help.
