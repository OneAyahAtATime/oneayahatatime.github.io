What you are buying

A licence to use One Ayah At A Time for 12 months, for one household. There is no limit on how many reciters you add or how many of your own devices you use. The licence is for your family, not for a school or madrasah to hand out — if you would like to use it with a class, please get in touch and we will work something out gladly.

The free week

Seven days with everything open, no card and no account. It is tracked on your own device and offered once per device, in good faith.

  • The subscription renews automatically every 12 months until you cancel, at the price you signed up at — we will tell you in advance if that ever changes

Your licence key

Your key is for your household. Please do not publish it or pass it around beyond your family. Keys shared publicly may be deactivated. The app checks the key with Gumroad about once a week; if the subscription has genuinely ended, the app locks — but it never deletes anything.

Your progress belongs to you

Everything you record is stored on your own device first, and losing access to the app never removes it. You can save it to a file whenever you like, and load it back whenever you need.

Once you hold a licence key, your progress is also carried between your own devices, filed under a scrambled fingerprint of that key with no name or email attached. Our privacy page sets out exactly what that means. Removing a reciter removes them everywhere, and you can ask us to delete your family's records at any time.

During the free week nothing is stored anywhere but the device you started it on, so for that week keeping a copy is your responsibility: clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or moving to a new device will lose it if you have not saved a copy first.

But it is provided as is: we cannot promise it will be free of faults or never interrupted, and we are not liable for loss beyond what you paid us in the previous 12 months. Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer law where you live.
