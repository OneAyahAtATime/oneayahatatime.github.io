About

Last updated 17 August 2026

Memorizing the Quran is a long road, and it is easy to lose sight of how far you have already come. One Ayah At A Time is somewhere to see it.

Every surah is a book on a shelf. You mark one when you begin it, again as you learn it, again while you are keeping it strong — and one more time when you can say it from memory. Slowly the shelves fill in, and a year of quiet effort turns into something you can look at.

We are a small, Muslim-owned family business. This app was built for our own household first, because nothing we could find felt like it was made for a family memorizing together. If you write to us, one of us reads it — there is no support desk here.

Made for your whole household

One payment covers everyone under your roof. Add as many reciters as you like — each one keeps their own books, their own dates and their own certificates, so nobody's progress is tangled up with anybody else's. There is no cap on how many people, because families are not all the same size.

What you can do with it

  • Begin anywhere — all 30 Juz are here, across eight illustrated pages, so you can start from wherever you already are
  • Say how it's going in your own words — learning this, doing muraja’ah (revision — going back over something you already learned so it stays strong), or in my heart
  • Note exactly which ayahs you are on, book by book, so you never lose your place
  • Print a certificate for every Juz — and a Khatm al-Qur’an certificate when the whole Quran is in your heart
  • Pick up where you left off, anywhere — a tablet downstairs, a phone upstairs, the same books on both
  • Keep it private — no accounts, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing filed under your name

Try it before you decide

Seven free days with everything open, including the certificates. No card, no account, nothing to cancel. After that it is $40 a year for your whole family — or $25 if you already have Spelling Quest or Muslim Kids Checklist.
