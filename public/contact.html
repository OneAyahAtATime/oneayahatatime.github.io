We are a small, Muslim-owned family business, so your message reaches a person rather than a queue. We usually reply within two business days.

If something has gone wrong

Tell us whatever you can — even “it just stopped working” is enough to start with. These three things help us find it faster:

  • what you were doing when it happened
  • which browser and device you were using
  • the last six characters of your licence key — never the whole key

Questions we hear a lot

“It says my subscription has ended.” Take a breath — nothing has been deleted. Every book, date and certificate is still on that device and comes straight back. The usual cause is a card that expired or a payment that did not go through, so check your email from Gumroad, who handle our payments. If you think it is a mistake, write to us and we will fix it.

“I'm getting a new phone.” Open One Ayah At A Time on it and enter the same licence key. Your reciters and their books arrive on their own within a moment — that is what the key is for.

The one time you need to carry progress across by hand is during the free week, because nothing about the trial is stored anywhere but the device you started it on. Tap Save a copy of your progress at the bottom of the screen to get a small file, then Load a saved copy on the new device. It is a good habit either way, and if it is already too late, write to us anyway — we will help however we can.

“Can we use it on more than one device?” Yes — the same key unlocks it on every device in your household, and every one of them stays up to date with the others.

“Can I cancel?” Any time, from the link in your purchase email, and you keep access until the end of the year you have paid for. See our refund policy.
