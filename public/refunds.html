Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 17 August 2026

Try before you pay. Seven free days, everything unlocked, no card needed. We would much rather you found out it wasn’t for you before spending anything.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time using the link in your purchase email from Lemon Squeezy. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Your progress stays on your device either way.

Refunds

  • Within 14 days of a payment — email us and we will refund it, no explanation needed. That applies to a first purchase and to a renewal.
  • A renewal you did not expect — if you meant to cancel and the renewal caught you out, tell us and we will refund it, even past 14 days.
  • Something is broken — if the app will not work for you and we cannot fix it, we will refund you in full whenever that happens.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Lemon Squeezy, and usually appear within a few business days.

How to ask

Email oneayahtime@gmail.com with the email address you used to buy, and the last six characters of your licence key if you have it — never the whole key. That is all we need.

If you live somewhere with stronger rights

Consumer law in some countries gives you a cancellation right beyond what is written here. Nothing on this page limits it — whichever is more generous to you, applies.
