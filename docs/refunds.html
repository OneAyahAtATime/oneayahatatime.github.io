Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 18 August 2026

Try it before you pay anything. Seven free days, everything open, no card needed. We would much rather you discovered it wasn't for you before spending a penny.

Cancelling

Cancel any time using the link in your purchase email from Gumroad. That stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the year you have already paid for, and your progress stays on your device either way.

Refunds

Try it free for seven days before you pay anything — that is how we would like you to decide whether this is for you. Beyond the trial, we do not offer general refunds, with two exceptions:

  • A renewal you did not mean to pay — if you meant to cancel and it slipped past you, tell us and we will refund it
  • Something is broken — if the app will not work for you and we cannot fix it, we refund you in full, whenever that happens

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Gumroad, and usually appear within a few business days.

How to ask

Email oneayahtime@gmail.com with the address you bought with, and the last six characters of your licence key if you have it — never the whole key. That is all we need. You do not have to explain yourself.

If you live somewhere with stronger rights

Consumer law in some countries gives you a cancellation right beyond what is written here. Nothing on this page limits it — whichever is more generous to you, applies.
