About

Last updated 17 August 2026

One Ayah At A Time is an illustrated tracker for memorizing the Quran. Every surah is a book on a shelf. Color one in when you start it, mark it as you learn it, review it, and finally as something that is in your heart — and print a certificate when a Juz is finished, or when the whole Quran is.

Who it is for

Anyone memorizing the Quran, at any age, and anyone helping someone else do it. One payment covers a whole household, with as many reciters as you like — each with their own books, their own dates and their own certificates.

How it works

  • Eight illustrated pages cover all 30 Juz — 114 surahs in total.
  • Four gentle statuses: not started, learning this, doing muraja’ah (revision — going back over something you already learned so it stays strong), and in my heart.
  • Notes per book for which ayahs you are on right now.
  • Certificates for each Juz, and a Khatm al-Qur’an certificate for the whole Quran.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. Your progress is stored in your own browser.

Try it first

Seven free days, everything unlocked, no card and no account. After that it is $40 a year for the whole family — or $15 if you already have Spelling Quest or Muslim Kids Checklist.
