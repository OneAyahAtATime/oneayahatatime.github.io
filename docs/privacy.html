Privacy

Last updated 17 August 2026

The short version. We have no accounts and no advertising, and we do not track you. What you record about your memorization is yours — it is saved on your own device, and we never see it.

What stays on your device

  • The nickname you give each reciter
  • Which books you have marked, their status, the dates and your favourite surahs
  • Your ayah notes and which days you practiced
  • Your licence key, and whether the free week has started

All of it is kept in your browser's own storage. None of it is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it — from your device and from existence, because we never had a copy. That is also why we give you a Save a copy of your progress button, and why it is worth using before you switch devices.

What does leave your device

One thing: when you unlock the app, and about once a week afterwards, your licence key is sent to Lemon Squeezy to check it is still valid. Nothing travels with it — no name, no email, no progress, no reciters. If that check cannot be made, the app carries on as normal.

If you subscribe, Lemon Squeezy collects your payment and billing details directly. They are the merchant of record and their privacy policy covers that information. We can see order records in their dashboard; we never see your card details.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which like any web host records requests to its servers.

Cookies

We set none, and there are no tracking scripts, pixels or advertising networks here. Nothing to consent to, and no banner to dismiss.

Everyone in the household

The app is for anyone memorizing the Quran, at any age, and families often use it together. Because it asks for nothing but a nickname and keeps everything on your device, we do not knowingly hold personal information about anybody. Please use a nickname rather than a full name.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data are difficult to exercise against us, because we hold almost none: your progress is already entirely in your hands, and clearing the app's data removes it. For anything Lemon Squeezy holds about a purchase, contact them or write to us and we will help.
