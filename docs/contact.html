If something has gone wrong

Tell us whatever you can — even “it just stopped working” is enough to start with. These three things help us find it faster:

  • what you were doing when it happened
  • which browser and device you were using
  • the last six characters of your licence key — never the whole key

Questions we hear a lot

“It says my subscription has ended.” Take a breath — nothing has been deleted. Every book, date and certificate is still on that device and comes straight back. The usual cause is a card that expired or a payment that did not go through, so check your email from Lemon Squeezy, who handle our payments. If you think it is a mistake, write to us and we will fix it.

“I'm getting a new phone.” Your books live in the browser you have been using, so they need carrying across. On the old device, tap Save a copy of your progress at the bottom of the tracker — you will get a small file. On the new one, tap Load a saved copy and choose that file. Everything comes back exactly as it was, for every reciter.

Worth doing before you clear your browser, too. And if it is already too late, write to us anyway — we will help however we can, though we cannot bring back something that was never sent to us. We are working on making this automatic.

“Can we use it on more than one device?” Yes — the same key unlocks it on every device in your household.

“Can I cancel?” Any time, from the link in your purchase email, and you keep access until the end of the year you have paid for. See our refund policy.
