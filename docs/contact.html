We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

What to include

If something has gone wrong, it helps enormously if you tell us:

  • what you were doing when it happened,
  • which browser and device you were using,
  • and the last six characters of your licence key — never the whole key.

Common things

“It says my subscription has ended.” Your books are not deleted — they are still saved on that device and come straight back. The usual cause is a card that expired or a payment that did not go through; check your email from Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. If you think it is a mistake, email us and we will sort it out.

“I have a new phone / I cleared my browser.” Progress is stored in the browser itself, so it does not follow you automatically. Use Save a copy of your progress at the bottom of the tracker on the old device, then Load a saved copy on the new one. If it is already too late, email us — we will help however we can, though we cannot recover what was never sent to us.

“Can I use it on more than one device?” Yes. The same key unlocks the app on each device in your household.

“Can I cancel?” Any time, from the link in your purchase email. See our refund policy.
